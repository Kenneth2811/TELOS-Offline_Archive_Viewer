Complete Platform Overview

Built to Streamline School Operations

TELOS is a complete management solution built to bring together student records, staff management, attendance, assessments, report cards, timetables, and administrative operations into one simple platform. The goal of TELOS is to reduce paperwork, improve accuracy, and give schools better tools for decision making.

Student Management

Comprehensive directory tracking student profiles, guardians, medical data, and classes.

Staff Management

Maintain staff records, designations, credentials, qualifications, and emergency contacts.

Attendance Tracking

Monitor attendance, term days opened, present/absent logs, and generate summaries.

Assessment Management

Input test scores, projects, and examinations with automated grade scaling and computations.

Report Cards

Generate standardized terminal performance report cards with one-click PDF export.

Timetable Management

Organize class schedules, exam timetables, and weekly teacher duty rosters cleanly.

Administrative Control

Robust multi-role permissions, user setups, academic year configurations, and student promotions.

Document Submissions

Teachers submit weekly lesson notes, termly forecasts, and examination papers for secure administrative review.

Support

Admin Quick Start Guide

New to TELOS? Here's the fastest path to getting your school fully set up as an Administrator.

1

Sign In As Administrator

Log in with your admin email and password. Only the Administrator account can access the school-wide Dashboard, Settings, and school-wide configuration.

2

Complete Academic Setup

Under Settings → Academic Setup, set your School Type, Academic Year, Terms, and Grading System first — everything else builds on this.

3

Create Classes & Streams

Set up each class and stream your school runs (e.g. JHS 1A, JHS 1B) under Classes & Streams Management.

4

Add Staff & Assign Roles

Add teachers in Staff Directory, then assign each one as a Class Teacher and/or Subject Teacher under Subject Configuration & Assignment.

5

Add Students

Add students one at a time, or download the CSV/Excel template in Student Directory to bulk-add your whole roll at once.

6

Build Timetables

Set up each class's weekly timetable under the Timetable tab. Staff automatically see the timetables for their own class and subjects.

7

Track Scores & Attendance

Once the term is running, teachers enter scores and attendance is logged. You can review progress anytime from the Dashboard.

8

Generate Report Cards

At the end of term, generate, preview and download report cards individually, in bulk, or from the archived history — all as ready-to-print PDFs.
